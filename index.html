Executive Summary

What management needs to know in one screen
Overall recommendation
CONDITIONAL — enter as a non-China sourcing & export partner plus CRO; build capacity only against an anchor offtake

Ketonitrile is the cost-dominant input of ketoprofen: the nitrile is 60–75% of ketoprofen raw-material cost and one hydrolysis step away from the API. India is the leading exporter of ketoprofen API by transaction count, yet the only bulk imports in the data — 6.40 MT — went to one company from two Chinese suppliers. The opportunity is a reliable non-China supply chain for Indian and European ketoprofen makers; the risk is a price war with integrated Chinese producers.

  • What it is. 2-(3-Benzoylphenyl)propanenitrile — a benzophenone carrying a 1-cyanoethyl group at the meta position. Hydrolysis of the nitrile gives ketoprofen in 90–98% yield in a published process 1CN101759556A2US 4,201,870. The same molecule is the Ph. Eur. Structure

Reading the structure. A benzophenone (two benzene rings joined by a ketone) carries a 1-cyanoethyl group, –CH(CH3)C≡N, at the 3-position of one ring. The CH carbon is a stereocentre; the article of commerce is racemic.

  1. Nitrile → acid: hydrolysis converts –C≡N into –COOH and gives ketoprofen directly 1CN101759556A.
  2. Stereocentre retained: hydrolysis leaves the stereocentre untouched, so enzymes that convert only one enantiomer (nitrile hydratase plus amidase) can deliver (S)-ketoprofen, i.e. dexketoprofen. Ketonitrile has been used as a substrate to study this selectivity, and the approach is patented for the simpler 2-phenylpropionitrile 18ChemicalBook21US 5,258,305.
  3. Benzophenone chromophore: the same UV-active core makes Impurity F easy to detect by HPLC in ketoprofen.

Verdict
CONDITIONAL — enter through sourcing, export marketing and CRO; manufacture only against an anchor offtake

Why not "Recommended": the observed Indian import market is ≈3.5 t/yr (≈US$ 0.14 MN/yr) bought by a single company directly from Chinese suppliers at falling prices (US$ 43 → 37.3/kg); a 5–8% trading margin on that volume is only ≈US$ 7–11k a year, and Chinese price aggression on APIs limits how far Indian buyers can absorb a premium 5Trade data (India)13The Tribune.

Conditions to proceed: (1) written interest from at least two Indian ketoprofen makers or one EU/Korean CEP holder; (2) a qualified producer delivering ≤ US$ 40/kg CIF India (domestic) or ≤ US$ 55/kg for export lots; (3) for any plant, ≥100 t/yr of contracted demand plus a permitted cyanide-handling site or tolling partner.
